## Role

A **reusable poor-world village plate** — the ground for Szene 01 & 03 of
`kurzfilm-01-emil-geht`, and reusable across future poor-world episodes. Wide, mostly
empty, so Emil reads small against it and the slow pan has room.

## Constraints

- **Register:** poor. **Palette:** ≤3 of oat `#d9ccaa`, clay `#b07a4f`, iron `#6f7466` +
  ink `#241f1a`. No gradients — flat gouache fills.
- **Composition:** 2–3 **thin, tall, slightly crooked** houses in the left/mid; the right
  two-thirds is **bare oat negative space + a low horizon** (room to pan onto the castle).
- Everything slightly off-vertical. Reusable, plain — repetition is a feature.
- Aspect: **16:9**, extra width on the right for the PAN-R.
