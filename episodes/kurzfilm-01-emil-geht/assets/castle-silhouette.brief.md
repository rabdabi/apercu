## Role

The **rich world, seen from far away**: a huge, symmetric castle on the horizon,
revealed by the PAN-R in Szene 03. It plants the poor↔rich thesis without yet spending
the full rich palette — from Emil's distance it is mostly a **silhouette**.

## Constraints

- **Register:** rich, but **distant** — read as a dark/gold silhouette against a pale sky,
  not a detailed interior.
- **Palette:** ink `#241f1a` + gold `#c9a13b` (edge light) on the oat sky; a whisper of
  burgundy `#7a2233` allowed. Still flat.
- **Composition:** **symmetric, centred, monumental** — towers, ornament suggested by
  silhouette only. Sits on the same low horizon as `village-plate-a` (align the horizon).
- Emil, when composited, stays small in the lower-left — the castle dwarfs everything.
- Delivered as a **transparent-background element** to layer over the sky/plate.

## Prompt

```
A huge symmetric medieval castle on the far horizon, seen as a flat SILHOUETTE from a
great distance. Illuminated-manuscript flatness, imperfect ink edge. Mostly dark ink
mass with a thin gold #c9a13b rim-light and a faint burgundy #7a2233 accent; pale oat sky
behind. Monumental, centred, ornamental only in outline. Low horizon line.
NEGATIVE: no interior detail, no gradients, no 3D, no perspective realism, no extra
colours, no people, no text, not asymmetric.
```
