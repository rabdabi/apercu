# Reference analysis — Studio Loop (studioloop.com.br)

Visual study conducted in-browser at desktop and mobile (375px) widths. This is the
third reference alongside White Desert (story structure) and Anveril (information
precision). Studio Loop's contribution is **visual character**: art-direction-first
thinking, collage, playful typography, non-linearity, and the loop motif.

> **Rule for us:** study the principles, copy nothing. No blobby logo, no brown/orange
> palette, no keychain collages. We translate each principle into our own paper-white ×
> rubric-red identity below.

---

## What was observed (concretely)

### Intro / preloader
- Full-screen warm **dark brown** ground with an organic, blobby liquid "loop" wordmark
  in orange. A moment of pure graphic identity before content — brand as a held image.

### Services index (dark)
- An oversized numbered list: `(01) Video & Social Content`, `(02) Design`,
  `(03) 3D & Motion`.
- Tiny **`(01)` markers** in a muted lavender contrast against **gigantic** headlines.
- **Type mixing inside one line:** grotesk ("Video & Social") next to **italic serif**
  in orange ("*Content*"). A single word carries the colour and the serif — typography
  as image.
- **Hand-drawn rough horizontal rules** separating rows (imperfect ink, not hairlines).

### Manifesto (light chapter)
- Ground flips to warm **cream/paper**, dark brown text — a deliberate light chapter
  after the dark intro. Ground itself is a chapter device.
- Body statement mixes grotesk with italic serif; an accent word ("*solver*") is
  underlined as a link.
- **Scattered orange dots** float across the composition — a loose orbital/constellation
  motif, not a grid.
- Editorial metadata line: `[ FROM BRASIL TO EVERYWHERE ] ————————— 2026` — tiny
  bracketed label left, long rule centre, year right. Pure Anveril-grade precision,
  Studio-Loop warmth.

### Work index
- Project titles as large grotesk headings ("Schoolhouse", "Food52"), stacked as a list.
- Each title sits over a **full-bleed, warm, film-graded cinematic still/video** — the
  media does the talking; the type labels it.

### Mobile hero (375px)
- A **physical-object collage** photographed: keychains, printed cards, a booklet
  ("Deep culture, living language" in red script), yellow cards, lilac scribbles, orange
  rubber charms, a die-cut star. Tactile, cultural, hand-made.
- The word **"studio" set on a curved/orbital path** (letters arced), "*and* culture."
  in mixed italic-serif + grotesk beneath.
- Fixed **circular menu button** top-right; wordmark top-left. A peeling **folded-corner**
  detail in the top-right of the page on desktop — a small playful surprise.

### System-level takeaways
- **Ground alternates** dark ↔ light as narrative punctuation.
- **Numbering is real structure** ((01)(02)(03)), not decoration.
- **One accent colour**, used confidently on single words and objects, never diluted.
- **Imperfect marks** (rough rules, scribbles, object photography) keep it from feeling
  digitally sterile.
- **Type is graphic material** — serif/grotesk mix, colour on one word, text on paths,
  huge scale vs tiny metadata.
- Restraint underneath the play: consistent wordmark, consistent menu, legible copy.

## Translation to the Aperçu identity

Each Studio Loop principle → our version (paper-white ground, warm near-black ink,
**rubric red** everyday accent, **signal lime** as rare full-bleed jolt, Space Grotesk /
Newsreader / Space Mono).

| Studio Loop principle | Our translation |
| --------------------- | --------------- |
| Warm dark ground + orange | Warm **paper** ground + **rubric red**; lime reserved for rare full-bleed chapters |
| Blobby liquid logo | Keep the AP/ER/ÇU wordmark + aperture mark — no organic blob |
| `(01)` markers vs huge type | Space Mono `(01)` / `[ 003 ]` metadata against enormous Space Grotesk |
| Grotesk + **italic serif** in one line | Space Grotesk headline with a **Newsreader italic** accent word in rubric red |
| Hand-drawn rough rules | Generated slightly-imperfect SVG rules (subtle, not cartoonish) |
| Ground flips dark ↔ light | Our colour-interruption chapters: paper → full-bleed dark / lime / red → paper |
| Scattered orange dots | Our **dot/line diagram grammar** (M) — editorial constellation, not HUD |
| `[ LABEL ] —— 2026` metadata | Reuse verbatim in our voice: `[ SIGNAL / RAUSCHEN ] —— 12.09.2026` |
| Full-bleed film-graded stills | Our `AmbientClip` / `MediaFullBleed`, warm-graded generated media now |
| Object collage | Our `MediaCollage` as punctuation — generated fragments now, real later |
| Text on a curved path | Reserve for one signature moment (e.g. the Story Loop label), used once |
| Folded-corner surprise | One small recurring playful detail, tasteful, not on every card |

## What we explicitly will NOT take

- The brown/orange palette, the liquid logo, keychain/sticker collages, the studio's
  copy or layouts, its exact animations.
- Playfulness that undercuts a serious investigation. Our subjects (power, money,
  attention) demand that collage and motion **reinforce meaning**, never decorate.

## Net: what Studio Loop adds to our synthesis

Permission to make **type behave as image**, to **alternate the ground** as chapter
punctuation, to use **one confident accent** on single words, to let **imperfect marks
and collage** interrupt the grid — all held inside a strict, legible system. Combined
with White Desert's sequence/silence and Anveril's precision, on our paper-white ×
rubric-red identity.
