# WORLD / POOR REGISTER

> How a scene is drawn when it belongs to the Commons. Poverty is a *drawing style*,
> not a set of props.

STATUS / CANON · UPDATED / 11.09.26

## Feeling

Sparse, honest, a little cold. Room to breathe. The eye rests on one or two things.

## Rules

- **Colour:** 2–3 from the poor-world palette + ink (`world/palette.md`). Never more.
- **Negative space:** large empty areas of bare oat/parchment are correct. Do not fill.
- **Line:** rough ink outline, visible weight variation, occasional broken edges.
- **Buildings:** thin, tall, slightly leaning. Repeated modular houses are fine and
  wanted — the same three houses redrawn is a feature, not laziness.
- **Geometry:** everything slightly crooked. No perfect verticals.
- **Shading:** none, or a single hatch. Flat fills only.
- **Backgrounds:** may be reused across scenes and episodes. Poverty repeats.
- **Population:** few figures. Emptiness reads as scarcity.

## Motion in the poor register

- Small movements. Characters often hold still.
- Walk cycles are plain, on 2s (see `world/motion-language.md`).
- Cuts are hard; the camera barely moves.

## What it means

The poor register gives the audience space and quiet, so that when the story cuts to the
rich register, the density hits physically. Poor is not "sad" — it is *clear*.

## Canon checks (for `/apercu-canon`)

- [ ] ≤ 3 palette colours + ink in any single poor-world frame.
- [ ] Visible negative space present.
- [ ] No gradients, no soft digital shadows.
- [ ] Lines are imperfect, not vector-clean.
