# WORLD / CAMERA GRAMMAR

> How the camera behaves. The camera is an editor, not a performer.

STATUS / CANON · UPDATED / 11.09.26

## Default

Locked-off, frontal, slightly flat — like a painted stage. Most shots do not move.

## Allowed moves

- **Slow pan** across a scene to reveal scale (a banquet table, a village).
- **Slow push-in** to land on a face or an object at a beat.
- **Held wide** for establishing; **held medium** for dialogue; **held close** for the
  brow-acting.

## By register

- **Poor world:** camera barely moves. Hard cuts between locked frames. Wide, empty.
- **Rich world:** slow, deliberate moves are allowed and encouraged — the long push down
  the table. Movement here signals weight and time.

## Forbidden

- No handheld, no shake, no whip pans.
- No parallax spectacle, no 3D camera swoops.
- No zoom for its own sake — a push must land on a story beat.

## Shot vocabulary (for shot lists)

`WIDE` · `MED` · `CLOSE` · `INSERT` (object) · `PAN-L/R` · `PUSH` · `CARD` (title).
`/apercu-storyboard` emits shots using these tokens.

## Canon checks (for `/apercu-canon`)

- [ ] Every camera move is slow and lands on a beat.
- [ ] Poor-world scenes stay mostly static.
- [ ] No forbidden moves specified.
